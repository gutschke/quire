them into generated PDFs (text-searchable, deterministic, Letter and

first use of the Print PC sheet feature.

  LiberationSans-Bold.ttf      — sans bold, headers / emphasis
  LiberationSerif-Regular.ttf  — serif, body prose / backstory
  LiberationSerif-Italic.ttf   — serif italic, markdown emphasis
