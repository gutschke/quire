## ★ A. Magic-discovery arc (highest priority — #398 / #404 / #407 + firewall)
The biggest + most firewall-sensitive change. Walk the full arc with
**2 players** (PC-A, PC-B).

- [ ] **A1 — Accidental (pre-Realization):** on each player's Rail there
  is **no magic/casting section at all** (no "The Quiet" card, no tax
  line). A player must not even know casting exists yet.
- [ ] **A2 — Realization moment (PC-A only):** DM marks Realization on
  PC-A. On **PC-A's** screen a one-shot **"The world tilts."** treatment
  appears on the casting card *at the moment it flips*, then settles into
  the steady "The Quiet" card ("You can shape the Quiet…"). It reads as a
  quiet/ominous beat (NOT celebratory) and **auto-clears after ~6s** (no
  dismiss button).
- [ ] **A3 — Firewall (the load-bearing check):** **PC-B's screen shows
  NOTHING** — no "world tilts," no casting card, no hint that PC-A
  realized. Each PC realizes on its own schedule.
- [ ] **A4 — Tax active:** PC-A's casting card shows the amber **"Trying
  too hard — −2 to casts you reach for. Ease off and it passes."** It
  must NOT name a release condition or show a session countdown.
- [ ] **A5 — Release tax (DM):** the amber −2 line disappears on PC-A;
  the steady casting card remains.
- [ ] **A6 — Reload safety:** reload PC-A mid-Tax → the "world tilts"
  moment does NOT replay (only the steady card shows). If PC-A controls
  multiple PCs, switch the Rail to a not-yet-realized PC → no residue.
